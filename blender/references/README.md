## Image prompts

### Hero character sheet

Original cream-fur cat character for a premium interactive Parisian cat-years experience, Japanese hand-painted anime film sensibility mixed with mid-century French children's picture-book elegance, correct feline anatomy and elegant seated posture, natural tapered face and muzzle, small inset jade-green almond-shaped eyes, full triangular furred ears, soft cheek-fur tufts, long fluid tail, slim front legs, compact back haunches and grounded paws, a narrow dusty-rose silk bow with antique gold bell. Matte three-tone cel shading with delicate thin ink outlines and warm painterly gradients, neutral ivory background. SAME CHARACTER front, profile, back and three-quarter views, orthographic model sheet, no text, no copyrighted characters, no big plastic eyes, no grinning human mouth, no visible joins between balls.

### Four age checkpoints

Four matching views of SAME original cream/jade/rose cat age 0, 2, 10, 25 years, same ear marks and collar design, identical camera/scale reference and shared ground line. 0 year tiny round kitten at 43% adult size with large relative head, 2 years tall slim adolescent with youthful pose, 10 years serene elegant healthy adult, 25 years same cat with only subtle silvering at cheeks and slightly relaxed posture. Consistent model topology, natural cat expressions, hand-painted anime cinematic styling, no text.

### Balcony background colour script

Six matching wide frames of a Paris apartment balcony with roses, wrought iron rails, distant Eiffel Tower and warm velvet sill at sunrise, bright day, golden hour, sunset, twilight and starry night. SAME perspective and composition, empty centre for 3D cat composite, soft atmospheric perspective, Japanese animated-film painted backgrounds and delicate vintage French watercolour textures, warm window lights at night, no humans or logos.

### Shader target

Three-value matte cel shading on cream feline fur, very subtle warm fur variations and tapered silhouette fur tufts, jade eyes with natural pupils, soft ambient occlusion and rim light that changes from amber sunset to pale-blue moonlight, no metallic shine, no voxel fur, no giant eyeliner.

## Reference discipline

- The rendered character must remain internally consistent even if concept references differ panel-to-panel.
- Use genuine feline anatomy photographs as additional modelling references only if permitted by the image licence. Track source and permission in this folder.
- No copied Disney film frames or character models. Make something clearly original.
- Judge same-camera age checkpoints at 240 px width before trying to integrate it with the UI.
